WHAT THIS APP DOES THAT LOGGING APPS DO NOT
The user logs bowel movements (Bristol Stool Form Scale 1-7), pain, urgency and a daily summary, and registers the thing they are trying - medication, supplement, food change, exercise or habit.

The timeline is then divided into phases with explicit start and end dates (baseline / trying it / break). The app reports each phase's averages against the baseline's, with the min-max range beside every average and the number of recorded days behind it. Within one phase it also separates days marked "did it" from days marked "did not do it" and reports those two groups separately. Records are three-state (not logged / did it / did not do it), so a day that was never logged is never counted as a day the user skipped.

When the same observation target is tried in two or more completed phases, the reproducibility check compares each occurrence with the baseline or break phase immediately before it. It reports whether the recorded change moved in the same direction each time and shows every difference separately. Each comparison requires 7+ recorded days on both sides. It does not infer effectiveness, causality or medical significance.

We surveyed the Japanese App Store first (iTunes Search API; 1,035 retrieved, 225 with bowel or gut logging as their subject after removing games, toilet finders and unrelated results). Of those 225, apps comparing "days it was done" against "days it was not": 0. Two describe a before/after comparison - one around a marker (a point in time), one against the preceding rolling period - and neither compares periods the user defines with a start and an end date. Raw data available.

NO MEDICAL CLAIMS
The app states measured differences and nothing more. The only sentence it composes reads: "In the records, compared with the baseline, the average number of bowel movements is 1.4 per day lower (-58%)." It never says whether something is working and gives no diagnosis or advice. A difference becomes a sentence only once both sides have 7+ days of records. Settings > About directs the user to their physician or pharmacist.

PRIVACY AND CAMERA
No accounts, server, network calls, analytics or advertising. Data leaves the device only via Export or a transfer file, through the share sheet. The camera is optional (Settings > Observation targets), for a photo of one's own prescription; those images are re-encoded to drop Exif/GPS and are excluded from every export.

ATTACHED VIDEO

TO REACH THE COMPARISON ON A FRESH INSTALL
1. First screen: tap the primary button, create a plan.
2. History tab: tap any past date to open that day's editors; backfill about a week. Future dates are intentionally not tappable.
3. Record tab: start a second phase from the top card, then backfill several more days.
4. Compare tab: scroll to the second phase card. Both comparisons appear there.
5. To show the reproducibility check, repeat the same observation target in another completed phase after a baseline or break and record 7+ days on both sides of each comparison.
